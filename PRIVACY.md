# Privacy Policy — MD Quick View

**Last updated: 26 May 2026**

MD Quick View is developed by Turczynski Interactive Ltd.

## Data Collection

MD Quick View does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information. The app has no analytics, no telemetry, and no network access of any kind.

## File Access

The app reads only the Markdown file you choose to preview in Finder. No file contents are stored, logged, or sent anywhere. All processing happens locally on your device.

## Third Parties

MD Quick View does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or advertising networks.
